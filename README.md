## PayPal, card, bank transfer, or P2P?

The best route depends on what you value most.

### PayPal

PayPal is convenient when the option is available and your account is already funded. It also avoids entering card details directly into the exchange checkout. The tradeoff is limited regional availability and the fact that PayPal cannot currently be used for EUR withdrawals through OKX.

### Card

Card purchases can be quick, but the processing fee and exchange rate deserve close attention. OKX’s documentation says card fees vary by country of residence and that supported cards depend on the relevant regional product.

### Bank transfer

A bank transfer is often better suited to larger deposits or users who want a lower-cost funding route. For EUR SEPA deposits, OKX says it does not charge a deposit fee, although your bank may apply one. Standard transfers can take longer, while SEPA Instant depends on your bank and availability.

### P2P

P2P can provide more local payment choices. OKX states that P2P buy and sell transactions do not carry a trading fee, but the payment method, advertiser pricing, and external wallet or bank charges still affect the overall cost. The process also requires following the platform’s escrow and release instructions carefully.

For a first purchase, PayPal or card is usually simpler when available. For repeated funding, bank transfer may be easier to compare. P2P is more flexible, but it requires more attention during each transaction.

## Common mistakes when buying crypto with PayPal

### Assuming PayPal is available worldwide

It is not. Regional eligibility is the first thing to check.

### Ignoring the final quote

The amount paid and the amount of crypto received are more important than a single advertised fee. Review the full transaction preview.

### Confusing a deposit with a purchase

A PayPal EUR deposit does not automatically mean you have bought Bitcoin, Ethereum, or another asset. It only funds the cash balance. You still need to place the purchase or trade.

### Expecting PayPal withdrawals

OKX currently documents EUR withdrawals through SEPA bank transfer rather than PayPal. Plan the exit route before depositing a meaningful amount.

### Using a third-party PayPal account

The matching-name requirement is not decorative. A mismatch can prevent linking or trigger additional checks.

### Buying before checking transfer requirements

If you intend to move the crypto to another wallet, review the supported network, minimum withdrawal, and withdrawal fee before purchasing. A cheap purchase can become inconvenient if the asset is difficult or expensive to transfer.
